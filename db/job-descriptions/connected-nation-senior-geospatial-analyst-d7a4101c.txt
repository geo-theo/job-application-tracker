DESCRIPTION
The Senior Geospatial Analyst manages and performs analyses of geospatial data related to broadband access, adoption, and use in a geographic information system (GIS). The Senior Geospatial Analyst works extensively with broadband-related, environmental, and demographic information to provide analytical, interactive map and data visualization solutions for Connected Nation’s (CN’s) internal and external stakeholders. The Senior Geospatial Analyst supports geospatial databases and data warehouses to manage internal and project data. The Senior Geospatial Analyst develops scripts and workflow automations to optimize data processing, management, and analysis.

ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
•Develop hardcopy and interactive geospatial and mapping products through the development and analysis of data using the software tools and resources provided by Connected Nation.
•Develop and maintain custom geospatial applications and interactive mapping solutions.
•Develop and maintain databases and automation scripts for processing and loading data.
•Perform location-based data processing, including but not limited to geocoding, digitizing, data editing and aggregation, table creation, data conversion, and georeferencing.
•Analyze, calculate, and compile statistics from spatial analysis by geographic area to assist in qualitative and quantitative decision making.
•Collaborate and coordinate with the Geospatial team for efficiency and effective product development.
•Keep pace with geospatial technology advances and innovations through training and conference opportunities.
•Responsibilities are not all inclusive; additional duties may be assigned.

EDUCATION/EXPERIENCE
•Bachelor’s degree in GIS, geography, geology, planning, or related scientific field required with emphasis on GIS preferred.
•Five years’ minimum experience, or educational equivalent, in GIS required.

QUALIFICATIONS

•Python scripting and SQL proficiency.

•Must be proficient in Microsoft Office Suite, especially Microsoft Excel.
•Cultivates strong and collaborative working relationships with staff, while having the ability to handle sensitive and confidential datasets.
•Must be able to communicate effectively with all levels of staff.
•Strong analytical, organizational, and communication skills.
•Must be able to work independently and under pressure in a fast-paced environment.
•An extremely organized, disciplined, self-directed, hands-on, and process-oriented individual who is not afraid of digging into details when necessary.
•Ability to manage and prioritize multiple tasks.
•Strong work ethic and unquestioned personal and professional integrity.
•High level of commitment to team and company values.
•Attention to detail, establishing priorities, and meeting deadlines.
•Willing to travel as needed.
•Valid state driver’s license required.
