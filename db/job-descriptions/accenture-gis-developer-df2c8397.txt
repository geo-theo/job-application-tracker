The work:

Develop, maintain, and update ArcGIS Online (AGOL) dashboards, map services, web layers, and reporting tools for coverage reinvestment activities.
Design and build web‑based GIS applications and visualization tools using JavaScript GIS frameworks (e.g., ArcGIS API for JavaScript, Leaflet, Mapbox, OpenLayers).
Implement GIS functionality within web applications, ensuring responsive, user-friendly, and accessible interfaces that support program reporting and operational needs.
Import, export, and organize large ArcGIS datasets and associated metadata while ensuring integrity, consistency, and adherence to geospatial data standards.
Process and analyze crowdsourced datasets and geospatial information to generate insights that inform AOI analysis, investment decisions, and program reporting.
Support creation of geospatial products such as maps, layers, attribute tables, dashboards, and documentation that meet FirstNet Authority accuracy and traceability requirements.
Assist senior GIS personnel with workflows, model runs, data preparation, and version control tasks for nationwide coverage planning.
Conduct routine validation and quality checks on GIS datasets to ensure completeness, accuracy, and compliance with program standards.
Maintain repository structures, file catalogs, and AGOL content for efficient retrieval and use of spatial products across engineering, business and program teams.
Provide day‑to‑day support for geospatial documentation needs, including updating SOPs, workflow diagrams, and materials for executive, technical, and program reviews.
 

Here’s what you need:

Must be a U.S. citizen with the ability to obtain Public Trust required.
Bachelor’s degree required.
Minimum of 6+ years of experience as a Geographic Information Systems (GIS) Analyst or Developer.
A graduate certification or advanced degree may offset one year of experience.
Experience establishing and maintaining ArcGIS Online (AGOL) mapping and reporting dashboards.
Professional experience developing GIS applications using JavaScript-based web mapping tools (ArcGIS API for JS preferred, but other JavaScript GIS platforms are acceptable).
GIS development experience must include creating, modifying, and deploying web GIS components such as maps, layers, widgets, and interactive geospatial tools.
Experience importing and exporting large ArcGIS datasets and associated information.
Experience using crowdsourced data to produce actionable insights for investment activities.
 

Preferred Qualifications:

Demonstrated knowledge and experience with a multi‑state or national public safety communications program or project.
Experience integrating GIS visualizations into web-based operational dashboards or enterprise applications.
Experience ensuring cross-platform compatibility and performance optimization of web GIS applications.
