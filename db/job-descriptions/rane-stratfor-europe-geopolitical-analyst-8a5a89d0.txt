RANE Europe Geopolitical Analyst

About the Role
As a member of RANE’s core team of analysts, you will be responsible for developing high-quality and forward-looking analysis related to significant political, economic and security developments in the Europe region, with a particular focus on both tactical and strategic security issues. You will also be responsible for ensuring that clients are well-served by proactively identifying geopolitical trends while conducting deeper research on key topics including but not limited to political, economic, social, military, energy, technology and trade dynamics. Analysts have direct engagement with clients by supporting inbound geopolitical inquiries and providing briefings. Analysts also play a critical role in our forecasting process and production of Key Forecast Questions and scenario building. You will be trained in the RANE geopolitical methodology and have the opportunity to partner and interact with colleagues across the broader RANE organization and gain exposure to a range of industries and risk topics, including cyber and information; physical safety and security; and legal, regulatory, and compliance.

Responsibilities
• Conduct geopolitical research and analysis on European countries, trends and developments leveraging open source information, as well as proprietary content/data sets, and in-house subject matter experts
• Produce daily written content, develop longer-form analysis, and provide briefings to current and potential RANE clients on European political, economic, security and energy issues
• Actively participate in daily RANE team meetings and forecasting process; responsible for updating quarterly risk ratings related to the region, as well as participating in ad-hoc team analytic projects

‍Key Skills and Competencies
• Creativity and clarity in constructing clear, logical arguments with supporting evidence; strong analytical and critical thinking skills
• Excellent writing, research, briefing, and editorial capabilities; strong command of English language and grammar
• Rigor and resourcefulness in conducting open-source research, to include that of local media outlets across the globe, social media, government and commercial data, and academic and trade publications. Experience with primary source research.
• Ability to work collaboratively and effectively across multiple teams and stakeholders; flexibility and ability to adapt quickly to changing requirements are critical
• Specific expertise on European Union policymaking, Germany, France and the United Kingdom is particularly desirable; expertise on Central and Eastern Europe is also desirable
• Expertise on security issues including the analysis of military forces (including structure, capabilities and doctrines), weapon systems, tactics and strategies is highly desirable
• Well-rounded and in-depth understanding of any of the following is highly desirable: political economy, regional security and/or trends in energy, technology, and trade

Qualifications
• Master’s Degree preferred
• Bachelor’s Degree with demonstrated academic achievement required
• 3-5 years’ relevant work experience
• German, French or Russian language skills are highly desirable
• Exceptional oral and written communication skills
• Previous qualitative research and report-writing experience in a professional domain
• Experience with social media monitoring tools a plus
• Demonstrated sense of initiative and curiosity; willingness to ask questions and get answers
• Ability to meet deadlines and multi-task in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment
• Ability to function in a start-up atmosphere; comfort with ambiguity and change
