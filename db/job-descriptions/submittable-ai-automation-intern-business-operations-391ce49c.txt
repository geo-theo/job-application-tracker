About the Role
We're looking for an intern who already has real hands-on experience with AI tools like Claude and Gemini and is excited to put that experience to work on real business problems. You'll join our G&A team, partnering with People Ops, IT, Finance, and Accounting to spot repetitive, manual processes and turn them into AI-assisted or fully automated workflows.

This is a hands-on role from day one — you'll be embedded with real teams, solving real problems, and helping move active projects across the finish line. It's a great fit for a business-minded student who wants experience at the intersection of AI, operations, and process improvement, and who wants to see their work actually put to use.

What You'll Do
Partner with G&A, People Ops, IT, and Accounting to identify and develop AI automated solutions to support daily operations. 
Design, build, and pilot AI-powered workflows and tools using Claude, Gemini, and other AI platforms.
Help move existing AI and operations projects forward — from research and prototyping to testing and rollout.
Document new processes and create simple guides or playbooks so teams can adopt and maintain the tools you build.
Gather requirements from stakeholders across departments, test proposed solutions, and iterate based on feedback.
Track the impact of your work — time saved, errors reduced, processes streamlined — and report results to G&A leaders and key stakeholders. 
Take on ad hoc business operations projects and tasks as priorities shift across the G&A function.
