About this opportunity
Internships are educational hands-on opportunities that provide unique NASA-related research and operational experiences for undergraduate and graduate students (age 16 and up). To learn more, visit https://intern.nasa.gov.
This internship focuses on geospatial data curation and processing for irrigation mapping. The intern will coordinate with state agencies and other data providers, clean and harmonize vector and raster datasets, create label datasets, and prepare standardized satellite inputs to benchmark several geospatial foundation models, including Prithvi, TerraMind, Clay, Google AlphaEarth, TESSERA, And OlmoEarth. Desired skills, Python, geospatial libraries, GIS and remote sensing, data cleaning, and strong communication.
Familiarity with machine learning and deep learning and experience training or fine-tuning machine learning or geospatial foundation models (for example with PyTorch/TerraTorch) is considered a plus.
