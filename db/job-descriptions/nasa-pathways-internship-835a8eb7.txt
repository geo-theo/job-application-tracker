Explore the available Spring 2027 Pathways positions on this page and the NASA centers where they are located. Positions will start January 25, 2027.

To apply, identify the position(s) that align with your field of study and confirm that you have a qualifying major. Your major determines which position(s) your application is considered for. If your major qualifies you for more than one position, you may apply to each one. Use the corresponding link to apply through USAJOBS between September 21 and September 25.

When applying, you’ll be able to select up to two center locations. Only select centers that are hiring for that position. If you pick a location that is not listed for the position you are interested in, your application will not be reviewed by that hiring center.

For more on where NASA centers are located and the work they do, see NASA Centers & Facilities to learn more about the work each center performs in support of our mission. For additional guidance and resources to help prepare your application, see the Pathways “How To Apply” section.
