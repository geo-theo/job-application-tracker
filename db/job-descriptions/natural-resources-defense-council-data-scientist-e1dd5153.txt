Overview
NRDC is a non-profit environmental advocacy organization. We use law, science, and the support of 3.1 million members and online activists to protect the planet's wildlife and wild places and to ensure the rights of all people to clean air, clean water, and healthy communities. NRDC was founded in 1970 and our people helped write some of America's bedrock environmental laws, including the Clean Water Act and many of the implementing regulations. Today, our team of more than 700 lawyers, scientists, economists, policy advocates, communications experts, and others work across the United States and the globe from our offices in Bozeman, Montana; Chicago; New York; San Francisco; Santa Monica; Washington, D.C.; and Beijing.

 

NRDC is seeking a Data Scientist to work with the Science Office team in our Washington DC office.

 

Position Summary

 

The Data Scientist conducts statistical analysis, computation, geospatial analysis, and data visualization and mapping to support NRDC’s advocacy priorities, turning environmental data into rigorous, defensible findings. Working within the Data Science team in the Science Office, the Data Scientist leads discrete projects from intake through final product, with substantial mentoring and team collaboration, helping the team meet sustained, organization-wide demand for data analysis and visualization support. 

 

The position you are applying for is part of the bargaining unit represented by The Washington Baltimore News Guild (WBNG / The Newsguild-CWA Local 32035).  

Responsibilities
To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each essential duty satisfactorily. Reasonable accommodations may be made to enable qualified individuals to expand their knowledge and familiarity with certain data science tools used for these duties as part of their work at NRDC: 

Conduct statistical analysis of environmental data, such as pollution levels, species counts, and climate measurements, producing defensible findings that can withstand scrutiny from regulators, courts, and public review. 
Apply modern statistical methods, including approaches such as regression analysis, hypothesis and significance testing, and time-series analysis, to detect trends, quantify uncertainty, and strengthen evidence-based recommendations. 
Develop user-friendly, tailored resources such as interactive maps, dashboards, graphics, factsheets, and presentations to support NRDC advocacy priorities. 
Build and refine data visualizations and maps in tools such as ArcGIS Online and Tableau, making project-specific design decisions. 
Clean, validate, and manage data manually to ensure accuracy and credibility. 
Serve as project lead on data analysis and visualization requests, working directly with NRDC advocates, litigators, scientists, and external partners. 
Research, collect, and synthesize information relevant to data science requests received by the team. 
Manage projects across their full lifecycle from data acquisition, cleaning, analysis, visualization development, stakeholder review, revision, and quality assurance, to include project management pieces such as timelines, deliverables, and meeting agendas. 
Synthesize technical information and communicate findings clearly to non-expert audiences, including through written products such as NRDC blogs. 
Participate in internal and external meetings and activities of the Science Office as required. 
The above list of duties is not comprehensive but generally demonstrates the types of matters under this position’s responsibility. Other duties may be assigned. 

Qualifications
Minimum Education & Experience: 

Bachelor’s degree in statistics, data science, environmental science, or a related field, or an equivalent combination of education and experience, required.
2–4 years of related experience in data analysis and research, with experience in environmental policy and advocacy preferred. 
Skills, Abilities, Competencies: 

Proficiency with R or Python, and ArcGIS or Tableau, to conduct statistical analysis and develop data visualizations.
Demonstrated statistical expertise, with familiarity in modern methods.
Familiarity with SQL and data-wrangling
Proficiency in Microsoft Office, including advanced Excel skills preferred, Word, PowerPoint, and Outlook.
Strong writing and verbal communication skills, with demonstrated ability to translate technical information for non-expert audiences.
Ability to work independently and on quick timelines, managing multiple workstreams from kickoff to end-product design.
Ability to work collaboratively in teams toward shared outcomes.
Working understanding of key energy and environmental policies preferred.
Ability to travel to NRDC U.S.-based offices, approximately <20% annually.
