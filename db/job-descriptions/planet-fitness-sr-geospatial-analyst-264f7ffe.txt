Overview
Reporting to the Director, Geospatial Analytics & Data Science, the Senior Geospatial Analyst is responsible for supporting location strategy and business decision-making through geospatial analysis, market intelligence, predictive insights, and performance reporting.

 

This role partners with Development, Real Estate, Franchise Development, Operations, Finance, Marketing, and Enterprise Analytics to evaluate markets, identify growth opportunities, and deliver actionable insights that support site selection, market optimization, and long-term business growth.

 

The Senior Geospatial Analyst will analyze demographic, competitive, member, and market data; develop location intelligence that drives strategic real estate decisions; build and maintain geospatial dashboards and analytical tools; support market modeling and forecasting initiatives; and provide senior leaders with clear, data-driven recommendations through compelling storytelling.

 

The ideal candidate combines strong analytical capabilities, business acumen, intellectual curiosity, and a passion for translating complex geospatial data into actionable business insights and growth strategies.

 

This role follows a hybrid schedule and requires regular, in-person work at our Boston, MA or Hampton, NH office. Our hybrid model is M/T/W in office and TH/F are optional work-from-home. Candidates must reside within commuting distance of one of these locations. Fully remote work is not available for this role.

Responsibilities
Geospatial Analytics & Market Intelligence

Analyzes markets, trade areas, demographics, competitive landscapes, and member behavior to identify growth opportunities and support strategic decision-making.
Develops geospatial analyses, market assessments, and location intelligence that support site selection, market optimization, and long-term growth planning.
Creates analytical recommendations that identify opportunities to improve market performance, development strategy, and member accessibility.
Supports forecasting, market sizing, cannibalization analysis, white-space analysis, and other location-based analytical initiatives.
Develops and maintains geospatial dashboards, scorecards, KPI reporting, maps, and analytical tools that provide visibility into market performance.
Monitors key market and development metrics, identifies trends, and communicates insights to business stakeholders.
Ensures data accuracy, consistency, and integrity across geospatial reporting and analysis.
Cross-Functional Partnership

Co-owns the design, development, and execution of geospatial analytics and market intelligence strategies with Development and Real Estate leadership.
Partners with Strategy, Finance, Marketing, Operations, Franchise Development, and Technology teams to support enterprise initiatives through geospatial analysis.
Translates analytical findings into clear, actionable recommendations for technical and non-technical audiences.
Supports ad hoc analyses and business requests related to market expansion, site selection, territory planning, and strategic growth initiatives.
