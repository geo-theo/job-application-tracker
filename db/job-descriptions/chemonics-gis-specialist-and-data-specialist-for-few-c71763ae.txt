Seeking EOIs and Recommendations for a GIS Specialist and Data Specialist for FEWS NET 8 Knowledge Base

Chemonics' East and Southern Africa Business Unit’s FEWS NET 8 Knowledge Base team is conducting an internal and external recruit for a GIS Specialist and a Data Specialist for the newly awarded Famine Early Warning Systems Network (FEWS NET) 8 Knowledge Base project. These positions will be based within the Washington, DC-based home office (though U.S.-based remote options may be considered for well qualified candidates).

Created in 1985 in response to famines in East and West Africa, FEWS NET is an integrated set of activities funded by the United States Agency for International Development (USAID) that provides timely, accurate, evidence-based, and transparent food security early warning information and analysis. FEWS NET makes reports available and distributes them globally to governments, relief agencies, and other organizations engaged in humanitarian response and development programs.   

The eighth phase of FEWS NET began in 2024. The FEWS NET 8 Knowledge Base project will work in 30 countries over five years to create and update FEWS NET’s knowledge base about livelihood zones and market systems on which FEWS NET’s acute food insecurity early warning analyses are predicated, thereby ensuring that USAID’s Bureau for Humanitarian Assistance (BHA) can allocate emergency food assistance effectively and in accordance with humanitarian principles. 

Responsibilities and Requirements for each of the positions are outlined below:

GIS Specialist:

The full-time DC-based (remote eligible) GIS Specialist for FEWS NET 8 KB will oversee formatting of geographic datasets, geo-referencing of data, and GIS shapefile management.
