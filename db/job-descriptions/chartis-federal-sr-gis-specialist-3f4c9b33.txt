Position OverviewChartis Federal is seeking a Senior GIS Specialist to support our customer. The Senior GIS Specialist will lead advanced geospatial analysis, mapping, and data integration efforts in support of our customer’s mission. This position plays a key role in ensuring the customer has accurate, reliable geospatial insights to plan operations, optimize communications coverage, and maintain consistent site and frequency data across the enterprise.
Responsibilities
Lead GIS analysis and provide technical guidance for mapping operational areas, coverage zones, and site configurations.
Develop advanced models and workflows to support aviation/maritime mission planning, site data integrity, and coverage visualization.
Oversee standards for site naming conventions, coverage layers, and data accessibility to ensure staff can efficiently manipulate and apply geospatial products.
Mentor and guide junior GIS analysts, ensuring accuracy and consistency of deliverables.
Collaborate with mission staff, engineers, and program stakeholders to deliver geospatial solutions that support operational readiness.
Prepare technical reports, visualizations, and presentations for leadership.
Support compliance, operational, and planning documentation with GIS-driven data products.

Requirements
Bachelor’s degree in Geography, Geospatial Sciences, Engineering, or related field (Master’s preferred). 
Must have active DOD Secret clearance or DHS Tier 2 BI with up-to-date investigation.
6–9 years of professional GIS experience supporting engineering, communications, or federal programs.
Expert proficiency with ESRI ArcGIS, ArcGIS Pro, and geospatial databases.
Experience managing coverage data, site naming conventions, and integration with operational tools highly desire. 
Strong leadership, communication, and mentoring skills.
