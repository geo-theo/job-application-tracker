About this opportunity
Internships are educational hands-on opportunities that provide unique NASA-related research and operational experiences for undergraduate and graduate students (age 16 and up). To learn more, visit https://intern.nasa.gov.
NASA’s Disasters Response Coordination System (DRCS) is accepting applications for an internship at NASA Langley Research Center working with the DRCS team to explore topics such as:
1) Geospatial analysis: support the DRCS in geospatial analysis and application of Earth observations for disaster response, creation of jupyter notebooks, and automation of geospatial production processes.
2) Knowledge management: building dashboards and functionalities in Microsoft SharePoint for programmatic knowledge capture and sharing.
3) Product communication: identify gaps in current communication products, support the development of new materials for building awareness in a variety of audiences, and identify opportunities for more plain language product descriptions.

Interns will gain experience conducting research regarding Earth observation data products relevant to disaster response, GIS, general knowledge and information management, and communications as they work on projects to produce a variety of supporting materials including presentations, geospatial data visualizations, and communications materials. Additional skills to be cultivated are project management, communicating with both NASA and external organizations, exposure to post-disaster emergency response protocols, and leadership structures.
